Install on your phone

  1. Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as this computer.
  2. Tap the Download button above from your phone's browser.
  3. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask to allow installing from this source — allow it for your browser app, once.
  4. Tap Install, then Open. You'll see Void's normal app icon in your launcher — nothing hidden.
